The Seven

Listed in the order their evidence lands. Your work is checked by the sin after you, and Wrath is checked by Gluttony.

Every sin, every member

  • Understand the complete design. Anyone can be questioned on any part of it.
  • Draft the pattern justifications for the parts your diagram touches, and send them to Envy.
  • Review the pull requests of the sin before you.
  • Make meaningful commits across several days, not one dump at the end.
  • Post your daily report in the group chat.
  • Attend the weekly project check-ins.

The brief says to model behaviour before designing classes, and says important actions in the activity diagrams must trace to the class design. That is why the order runs this way.

Branches and commits

  • main is protected. Nobody pushes to it directly.
  • Branch per piece of work: sin/short-topic, for example greed/scenario-draft.
  • Commit message: [sin] area: what changed, for example [sloth] readme: add setup notes.
  • Diagrams: commit the source file and an exported PNG or SVG together.
  • Small commits on several days. GitHub history is marked evidence.

Pull requests and review

  • Open a pull request when a draft is readable, not when it is perfect.
  • Your reviewer is the sin after you. Nothing merges without their approval.
  • After approval, the author merges their own PR.
  • Any change to a merged diagram gets an entry in the decisions log in the same PR.
  • Wrath can block any PR that makes two diagrams disagree.

Daily report

Three lines in the group chat at the end of each working day.

Done:    what merged or moved today
Next:    what you pick up tomorrow
Blocked: who or what you are waiting on

Anything blocked for long goes to the group leader.

Decisions log entry

The five fields Task 7 asks for. One entry per meaningful change.

## D-⟨number⟩ · ⟨date⟩ · ⟨sin⟩

Before:     the original design or assumption
Change:     the change that was made
Why:        why the change was made
Diagrams:   which diagrams were updated
